# Car Insurance Analysis

## Overview

This project uses Excel to analyze a car insurance dataset containing customer demographics, vehicle information, claims, household income, and coverage characteristics.

The analysis focuses on organizing customer and policy data into useful summaries that can help reveal patterns in claims, customer segments, vehicle characteristics, and coverage.

## Business Questions

The analysis explores questions such as:

- How are insurance customers distributed across demographic groups?
- How do claim frequency and claim amounts vary across customer segments?
- What patterns exist across vehicle makes, models, years, and usage?
- How does household income vary across the customer base?
- How are customers distributed across coverage zones?
- Which characteristics can be used to better understand the insurance portfolio?

## Analysis Process

1. Reviewed the dataset structure and checked the fields required for analysis.
2. Prepared the data for consistent analysis in Excel.
3. Used formulas and PivotTables to summarize customer, vehicle, income, and claims information.
4. Compared different customer and policy segments.
5. Used dashboard visuals and filters to make the results easier to explore.

## Portfolio Statistics

Household income in the analyzed dataset ranged from approximately:

- **Minimum:** 45,004.91

These summary statistics provide a useful baseline for understanding the financial profile represented in the insurance portfolio.

## Analysis Areas

### Customer Profile

Customer demographics were explored using variables such as gender, marital status, education, parent status, and household composition.

### Claims

Claim frequency and claim amounts were analyzed to understand how claims were distributed across the portfolio and across different customer characteristics.

### Vehicle Profile

Vehicle make, model, year, color, and usage were used to explore the composition of insured vehicles and compare segments.

### Coverage

Coverage zones were incorporated into the analysis to examine how policyholders were distributed across coverage categories.

### Income

Household income was summarized and compared across relevant customer groups to add financial context to the portfolio.
